From flour to counter

Every loaf on the shelf went through the same night.

  1. The afternoon before

    Mix and wait

    Flour, water, salt and last night’s starter. Then the dough rests in the cold for eighteen hours.

  2. 4:00am

    Ovens on

    Joe lights the deck ovens and shapes every loaf by hand.

  3. 5:30am

    The first bake

    Sourdough first, then rye, then the tins and the trays of buns.

  4. 7:00am

    Doors open

    Still warm. The board goes up and the kettle goes on.

Thirty-nine years behind the same counter

Marta’s parents opened the shop in 1987 with one oven and a sack of flour on tick. The counter, the bell over the door and the habit of saving a loaf for whoever’s late have not.

Joe came as a Saturday boy and stayed. These days he does the nights and Marta does the mornings.

Do you make anything without gluten?

Not safely. Flour is in the air from four in the morning, so we would rather not promise what we can’t keep.

Can I bring my own bag or tin?

Please do. Cake tins get a ten per cent discount on whatever goes in them.

What happens to the bread that doesn’t sell?

Anything left at closing goes to the Calder Bridge food bank, and yesterday’s rye becomes Saturday’s bread pudding.

Do you do wholesale for cafés?

For five cafés within walking distance, yes. Use the cake form below and tell us what you need.

Order a celebration cake

Birthdays, weddings, a retirement at the bus depot. Give us a week’s notice and a rough idea, and Marta will ring you to work out the rest.
