Why we cut every joint by hand

slope 1 : 7 in hardwood

A machine-cut dovetail is uniform and a little loose, because the cutter has to clear its own path. A hand-cut joint can be fitted to the thousandth of the board it is in, and it closes tighter every winter as the wood moves.

It takes us about forty minutes a corner. We think the drawer you open twice a day for fifty years is worth it.

  • 1 : 7Tail slope for oak, ash and walnut
  • 20 mmTail length, the thickness of the pin board
  • 40 minTime to saw, chop and pare one corner

Before you book

5 questions

I have never used a chisel. Is that a problem?

No. Most people on the stool course have not. We start with how to hold a tool and how to sharpen it, and a maker stays at your bench all day.

What should I wear?

Closed shoes and clothes you do not mind getting oily. We lend aprons, ear defenders and safety glasses.

Can I change my date after booking?

Yes, free of charge up to 14 days before the course. After that we will try to fill your place and move you if we can.

Is the workshop step-free and accessible for wheelchair users?

The ground floor is step-free, with a level entrance from the lane and an accessible toilet. Two benches are height-adjustable; tell us when you book.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, for any amount or any course, valid for two years. Use the form below and choose “Gift voucher”.
