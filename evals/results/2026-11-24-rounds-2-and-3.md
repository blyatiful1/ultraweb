# Rounds 2 and 3, 2026-11-24

Follow-ups to `2026-11-24-blind-round-1.md`. Same model (the smallest Claude tier), same one-page static setup, same single grader. These rounds were not blind in the full sense: the plain and frontend-design builds were the ones already graded in round 1, so only the new ultraweb build in each comparison was unfamiliar. The bike-shop brief in round 3 is the exception: all three builds were new.

## Round 2

ultraweb rebuilt on the short sketch path (`references/sketch.md`), with stand-in photographs available and the builder told to look at its own screenshots.

| Brief | Grader preferred |
|---|---|
| code review | ultraweb |
| game launch | ultraweb |
| bakery, architecture, hotel, nonprofit | frontend-design ("it does something better") |

ultraweb: 6 of 6 homepages passed the gates, 232k tokens and 9 min 52 s on average. Four builds used stand-in photographs (architecture 8, hotel 3, bakery 2, nonprofit 1). The bakery's photo search ran out of its anonymous quota partway through.

|---|---|---|
| frontend-design, six briefs | 92 to 176 px | 0 to 93% (two full-bleed) |
| ultraweb round 2, the two it won | 80 and 128 px | 0 and 93% |

frontend-design pages also alternated strong dark and light grounds and drew something for almost every section. ultraweb's losing pages were one pale tint with small pictures.

## Round 3

Changes first: a verified `--band` colour pair from `palette.mjs`, `.band`, `.cover` and `.first-screen` in the kit, a gate that fails a home page whose first screen has neither a 72 px headline nor a picture over 40% of it, and a "Commit" section in `references/sketch.md`.

| Brief | Grader preferred |
|---|---|
| bike shop (new brief, three new builds) | frontend-design |
| bakery | an earlier build over ultraweb's new one |
| hotel | an earlier build over ultraweb's new one |

All three ultraweb builds passed the gates and cleared the first-screen check; the plain bike build failed ten checks and the frontend-design one seven. ultraweb used 210k to 270k tokens, frontend-design 179k, plain 123k. The bike-shop build got no photographs: the free search was out of quota for the address after the earlier rounds, and it drew the frames instead.

## Where this leaves the plugin

Across three rounds the grader preferred ultraweb's page for two of the seven briefs (code review and, from round 2, the game launch). Its measured lead is correctness: 14 of 15 homepages passed the gates, against 0 of 14 for the other two arms. On looks, a 9 KB skill beat it at roughly 60% of the tokens, and three rounds of changes to ultraweb's guidance narrowed the gap without closing it.

Two things follow.

1. For the visual design of a one-page site, use the frontend-design skill. `references/sketch.md` now says so when that skill is installed. Whether frontend-design for the design followed by ultraweb's gates and fix loop keeps the look and gains the correctness has not been tested.
2. The stand-in photograph path depends on a free quota that six builds from one address exhausted in an afternoon. It needs a keyed source or a fallback before it can be relied on.
