# Five more packs, and a first trial of choosing, 2026-11-27

`2026-11-25-pack-test.md` ended with one pack that won where its character fitted the client and lost where it did not. This round adds five packs with different characters and tries the choosing step. Nothing here was graded by a person.

## Authoring

Each pack was authored by the top Claude tier from `packs/AUTHORING.md` and a character brief that fixed the direction, the clients, the type classes with a shortlist of families no sibling uses, the three colourways, the signature element and the three first screens.

| periodical (Quarterly) | $4.52 | 21 min | 26 | 18 | 6 |
| vitrine | $4.89 | 21 min | 36 | 18 | 7 |

$26.17 for the five at API list prices, priced per request, in a session that adds about 100K tokens of harness context to every agent. Fieldbook, the first pack, cost $4.57.

Every pack passed `packs.mjs check` and the accessibility and responsive gates on its catalogue as delivered, the text-overlap check included. The two sample raster pictures (in vitrine and nocturne) are drawings rendered by the author, not photographs.

## Choosing and building

Five new briefs were given to the smallest tier, one build each, with `references/pack-build.md` and all six packs to choose from.

| Brief | Pack chosen | Runner-up it named | Cost | Time | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a 60-seat jazz and folk cellar | nocturne | shopfront | $0.34 | 5 min 44 s | pass |
| a family cider orchard with a story to tell | periodical | fieldbook | $0.24 | 5 min 00 s | pass |
| a three-chair barber shop | shopfront | fieldbook | $0.26 | 4 min 08 s | pass |
| a jeweller working to commission | vitrine | fieldbook | $0.30 | 6 min 04 s | pass |
| an open-source uptime monitor | console | fieldbook | $0.23 | 3 min 34 s | pass |

- $1.37 for the five builds, $0.27 on average.
- All five passed the gates on an independent capture, and none shows text running into other text at 375, 768 or 1440 px.
- No builder changed its pack's stylesheet or added one. One added a three-line `<style>` block to pass a gate (see below).
- Every pack was chosen once, and each choice was the pack the brief was written for.

## Faults found and fixed

- **vitrine:** the display size came to 71 px at 1440, one pixel under the first-screen gate, so the builder had to patch its page. The pack's size is now 72.6 px there.
- **nocturne, vitrine and three others:** the catalogue preloaded one font or none, and one build had to add a preload to pass the layout-shift check. Every catalogue now preloads its two main faces, and `pack-build.md` tells the builder to keep the catalogue's `<head>`.
- **pack-build.md** forbade `style` attributes outright, while several packs' own patterns carry them (token colours on SVG parts). It now forbids adding any beyond those.

## What this does and does not show

It shows that each new pack survives a real build by the smallest tier, and that the manifest text is enough for that tier to tell six packs apart when the brief clearly belongs to one of them.

It does not show that the pages are good. No person graded them and nothing was compared against another way of building the same brief. The briefs were written with one pack in mind each, so the choosing was easy; a brief that sits between two packs, or suits none, has not been tried.

## Limits

One build per pack, no grader. The trial pages are not in the repository.
