# Brief: indie game launch page

## Prompt

We are two people making Lanternfall, a cozy puzzle-adventure about a lighthouse keeper who restores lights along a drowned coast. It launches on Steam and Switch in about six weeks. We need a launch page: what the game is, a trailer, screenshots, the wishlist button, a press kit for journalists, and a newsletter signup. People will arrive from social posts and from Steam.

## Strong result must show

- Immediate sense of the game's mood in the first viewport; the game's world drives the look.
- Trailer and screenshot presentation with real handling of media (placeholders must be honest).
- Wishlist and platform buttons prominent and correct; newsletter form with validation and confirmation.
- Press kit section: facts, assets list, contact, downloadable-looking structure.
- Motion that adds atmosphere without hurting scroll performance; reduced-motion respected.
- Not a generic dark neon game-site template.
