# Brief: architecture studio portfolio

## Prompt

Studio Halden is a six-person architecture practice in Rotterdam. We do housing, a few schools, and adaptive reuse of old industrial buildings. We want a portfolio site that shows eight or so projects properly, with drawings and photographs, who we are, and how to reach us. Clients are municipalities, housing associations and private developers; some of them are also looking for a job at the studio. We do not want it to look like a template.

## Strong result must show

- Project pages with a considered image sequence, plan or section, and facts (year, programme, area, status).
- Navigation between projects that suits browsing (index, filter by programme or year).
- Typography and layout with an editorial point of view; images given room.
- Placeholder imagery that is clearly handled with intent (drawings, tonal frames) rather than broken or stock.
- A studio, team and contact page that a client and a job applicant can both use.
- Performance is acceptable with image-heavy pages.
