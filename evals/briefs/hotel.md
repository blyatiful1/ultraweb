# Brief: small hotel with booking

## Prompt

Hotel Seeblick is a 14-room family hotel on a lake in the Austrian Salzkammergut. Rooms, a small spa, a restaurant with regional food, and walking and swimming right outside. Guests are couples and families, mostly from Germany, Austria and the Netherlands. We want a website where people can look at the rooms, understand what is around, and check availability and book directly instead of through the big booking portals.

## Strong result must show

- Booking widget reachable from every page: dates, guests, result with room types and prices (mocked is fine if labelled).
- Room pages with the details that decide a booking (size, bed setup, view, balcony, what is included).
- Sense of place and season; the lake, the walks, the food.
- Trust information: cancellation terms, address, directions, contact, reviews handled honestly.
- Mobile booking flow completes in few steps; touch targets and form labels are correct.
- Language switch present or clearly planned for DE, EN and NL.
