# Brief: non-profit annual report microsite

## Prompt

Wasserwege e.V. is a German non-profit that restores small rivers and streams. Our annual report for last year is currently a 40-page PDF nobody reads. We want a microsite that tells the year: what we restored, how many kilometres, who funded it, what it cost, and what comes next. Readers are donors, municipal partners and volunteers. Some will want the numbers, others the stories. We also need a way to donate and to download the full report.

## Strong result must show

- A narrative that works for both skimmers (key figures) and readers (stories).
- Numbers presented with context and sources; charts or maps that are legible and accessible.
- Financial transparency: income, spending, share to programmes, clearly laid out.
- Donate path that is obvious and trustworthy; PDF download present.
- Print-quality reading experience: line length, hierarchy, captions.
- Accessibility is visibly considered (contrast, alt text, chart alternatives).
