# Brief: neighbourhood bakery (pre-orders)

## Prompt

I run Brotkasten, a small bakery in Leipzig-Plagwitz. Sourdough loaves, rye, pretzels on Saturdays, and cakes to order. We bake at night and open at 7. People keep asking if they can reserve bread for the morning and order birthday cakes without calling us. I need a website with our opening hours, what we bake, and a way to pre-order for pickup. Mostly regulars and people who just moved to the area will use it, on their phones.

## Strong result must show

- Pre-order flow that works on a phone: pick items, pickup day and time, confirmation (can be mocked, must be honest about it).
- Real-feeling product detail (weights, flours, prices) rather than generic bread copy.
- Hours and address findable without scrolling on mobile.
- Imagery or illustration that is specific to bread, not stock-photo filler or empty gradient boxes.
- German-market details handled sensibly (Impressum/Datenschutz, EUR, 24h time).
- A look that belongs to a bakery in Leipzig, not a generic food template.
