# award canon

These entries are study notes without URLs. Check a site, its studio and its award before citing any of it to a user.

Load when: only when a site is cited by name in DIRECTION.md or a judge report, or when looking for precedent for one specific mechanism.
Reads: nothing. Writes: nothing.

Each entry gives the studio, the year it was studied in the award record, the idea, the signature mechanism, the patterns from `references/award/patterns.md` it shows, and where it cost itself points. Award tiers are given only where confirmed; jury scores, sub-scores and palette values from earlier drafts were removed because they could not be verified. Many of these sites are offline or rebuilt; the mechanism notes come from case studies and may not match the live site.

## 2024–2026

**hirotos.com "Signal Pole"** (Hiroto Sato, 2026). A portfolio built as one authored 3D scene whose single baked camera clip is the navigation; the runtime maps input onto the clip's playhead. Signature: travelling past a Japanese signal pole whose three lamps also set the color story. Patterns: Persistent Hero Object, Metaphor Engine, Scroll-as-Camera (mechanism only). Lesson: site-scale 3D can be an asset-authoring problem rather than a shader problem. Cost: reported without reduced-motion handling, with wheel input captured and no keyboard path, and with empty HTML per route.

**Messenger** (abeto, 2025). A small multiplayer 3D world in a browser tab with strict weight discipline. Signature: a tiny walkable sphere you loop around, with a camera you never touch. Patterns: Weight as a Feature, One Color Source (the whole palette baked into one small texture), Metaphor Engine, Instant Everything. Cost: canvas-only, so no DOM text, no accessibility tree, no static path.

**Lando Norris** (OFF+BRAND, 2025). An athlete's site that behaves like the sport: speed and momentum in every interaction, while still running a store and race hub. Signature: a hall of helmet designs that also drives the site's color. Patterns: Persistent Hero Object, Content-Derived Color, One Physics, Loader as Overture. Cost: very heavy media; a rotate-your-device wall on mobile.

**Igloo Inc** (Abeto with Bureaux; Awwwards Site of the Year 2024). A holding company's page reimagined as one continuous frozen world. Signature: portfolio projects encased in grown ice crystals. Patterns: One Material World, Scroll-as-Camera, Masked Cut, Loader as Overture. Lesson: coherence of one material beats effect count. Cost: interface text rendered in canvas, little crawlable content, thin message.

**Opal Tadpole** (Claudio Guglieri with Ingamana, 2024). Sells a very small webcam by making scale the whole story. Signature: the pinned product pushed through zoom and rotation on scroll. Patterns: Scroll-as-Camera without an engine, Kinetic Reveal Type, One Color Source. Lesson: restraint and product photography carried it, not novelty. Cost: single page, so little deep linking.

**Don't Board Me** (The First The Last, 2024). A pet-boarding service won on charm, warmth and legibility rather than WebGL. Signature: throwing a tennis ball to enter. Patterns: Loader as Overture, Scroll-as-Journey with illustrated scenes, One Physics. Cost: an entry gesture hurts conversion unless it is skippable and keyboard-operable.

## 2021–2023

**Lusion v3** (Lusion; Awwwards Site of the Year 2023). A quiet, near-monochrome studio shell around flawless real-time 3D that still loads fast. Signature: one hero object with real inertia; the cursor imparts momentum and scroll moves the camera through depth. Patterns: Persistent Hero Object, One Physics, Scroll-as-Camera, Type as Evidence. Cost: a full engine is out of reach for most commercial briefs.

**Noomo** (Noomo agency, 2023). Ukrainian embroidery and glass as a cultural narrative, so the 3D read as authored. Signature: a deliberate reverse scroll. Patterns: Scroll-as-Journey ("rooms in a hallway"), One Material World. Cost: reverse scroll fights habit; never use it on a task site.

**Mana Yerba Mate** (2023). A beverage shop turned into themed worlds per flavor without breaking a short path to checkout. Signature: flavor worlds dissolving into each other; a hidden game in the footer. Patterns: Scroll-as-Journey, Persistent Hero Object, Archive-as-Toy, Masked Cut. Cost: heavy runtime inside a shop platform.

**The Other Side of Truth** (The First The Last, 2022). A documentary site about the war in Ukraine where the message is the design. Signature: a toggle that re-narrates the page under two irreconcilable framings. Patterns: Interaction as Argument, Semantic Motion Only, Framed Data. Cost: heavy effects over documentary photography.

**KPR** (Resn, 2022). A fictional-world launch disguised as a product page. Signature: press and hold a character to reveal a lore layer. Patterns: Prove-It Gesture, Type as the Image, Fake Depth, Masked Cut. Cost: hold-to-reveal has no discoverability.

**Persepolis Reimagined** (Media.Monks for the Getty, 2022). A real-time reconstruction of a lost city. Signature: the loader's last frame is exactly the intro's first frame. Patterns: Loader as Overture, Scroll-as-Camera, Content-Derived Color. Cost: a game-scale asset pipeline; linear scroll is hard for assistive technology.

**Pangram Pangram** (Locomotive, 2021). A type foundry's shop that behaves like a magazine, showing fonts doing their job, built on DOM and CSS. Signature: a newspaper specimen that folds and reflows on scroll. Patterns: Type as the Image (earned, because type is the product), One Color Source, Scroll-as-Journey. Cost: heavy specimen imagery; light gray text that fails contrast.

**Prometheus Fuels** (Active Theory, 2021). A scroll-driven film for a product you cannot photograph. Signature: illustration and photography mapped onto 3D as one camera move. Patterns: Invert the Genre Palette, Fake Depth, Scroll-as-Camera. Cost: product facts buried in the story; the site is gone.

**Star Atlas** (Hello Monday / DEPT, 2021). A sci-fi game site that went light and warm instead of dark and neon, rendered as points of light. Signature: point-cloud depth of field racking focus on scroll. Patterns: Invert the Genre Palette, One Material World. Cost: weak accessibility and weight; replaced by a conventional site.

**Umami Land** (Media.Monks with Google, 2021). A virtual theme park for a cuisine where every structure comes from a dish or a tool. Signature: the world becomes more vivid the more you search. Patterns: Archive-as-Toy, Invert the Genre Palette. Cost: search-to-reveal hides content.

**Chungi Folio** (Zhenya Rynzhuk / Synchronized, 2021). An illustrated picture-book portfolio. Signature: page-turn route transitions. Patterns: Masked Cut, Type as the Image, Cursor as Narrator. Cost: broken display headlines and a custom cursor hurt assistive technology.

## 2017–2020

**Kode Sports Club** (Merci-Michel, 2020). A pre-opening gym page turned into a 3D world you explore as an avatar. Signature: a top-down map you drop into. Patterns: Shared-Element Lift, Content-Derived Color, Weight as a Feature (aggressive mesh compression). Cost: canvas world invisible to assistive technology.

**DARK: Official Netflix Guide** (MediaMonks with HAS.WORKS, 2020). The guide to a tangled time-travel show where the interface metaphor is the subject. Signature: the timeline made spatial. Patterns: Metaphor Engine, Progressive Spectacle Tiers (SVG and CSS baseline, WebGL on desktop), Interaction as Argument (spoiler filters). Cost: color as the only carrier of meaning; dark-only.

**Mammut Expedition Baikal** (Build in Amsterdam, 2020). A product range told as a documentary descent; products surface as the climber uses them. Signature: telemetry readouts (GPS, temperature, heart rate) riding the scroll as a field log. Patterns: Metaphor Engine, Scroll-as-Journey, Progressive Spectacle Tiers without WebGL. Cost: a campaign page, not a store: no filtering or comparison.

**Synchronized Studio** (Zhenya Rynzhuk, 2020). A portfolio that behaves like a showreel. Signature: very large type lockups with image transitions on hover. Patterns: Type as the Image, One Physics, Cursor as Narrator. Cost: custom cursor as the only affordance; media weight.

**Pioneer Corn Revolutionized** (Resn, 2020). A seed brand as one continuous scroll-scrubbed 3D film. Signature: scroll as the crop cycle. Patterns: Scroll-as-Camera. Cost: all type rendered in canvas with no DOM text; treat as a warning.

**Bruno Simon portfolio** (Bruno Simon, 2019). Drive a toy car around a diorama and crash into the work. Signature: the first key press knocks the 3D title over, so onboarding happens by consequence. Patterns: Metaphor Engine, Prove-It Gesture, Fake Depth (matcaps and baked shadows), Weight as a Feature. Cost: information requires driving; no static fallback. The current site is a later rebuild.

**Nomadic Tribe** (makemepulse, 2019). A studio greeting card in a hand-drawn comic language rendered in real-time 3D. Signature: one outlined, cel-shaded look on every asset. Patterns: One Material World, Prove-It Gesture, Semantic Motion Only. Cost: drag-and-hold with no affordance; story locked in the canvas.

**MA** (AQuest, 2019). A shop as four themed worlds mapped to product traits. Signature: color wipes between worlds that double as wayfinding. Patterns: Scroll-as-Journey, Masked Cut. Cost: the journey buries add-to-cart.

**The Cool Club** (WONDERLAND, 2019). A 54-card deck as one endless field of cards. Signature: a picked card lifts toward the camera and flips open. Patterns: Shared-Element Lift, Instant Everything, Metaphor Engine. Cost: crawlers saw an empty page.

**Active Theory v4** (Active Theory, 2018). A studio site as a real-time scene lit by the work it shows. Signature: scene light sampled from the playing video. Patterns: Content-Derived Color, Instant Everything. Cost: GPU-hot; navigation by flying and hidden gestures.

**Orano** (Immersive Garden, 2018). A nuclear-services company as flights over wireframe terrain. Signature: edge-only topography between sections. Patterns: Scroll-as-Camera, Weight as a Feature (many models kept small). Cost: navigation gated behind WebGL.

**Koox** (Blue Cheese, 2018). A small takeaway committed to one idea: watercolor food that moves like pigment. Patterns: One Material World (try SVG `feTurbulence` and `feDisplacementMap` first). Cost: spectacle outran the menu content.

**Frans Hals Museum** (Build in Amsterdam, 2018). A museum merger expressed as old meets new in loud color blocks and kinetic type. Signature: browse the collection by mood, color or at random. Patterns: Archive-as-Toy, Type as the Image. Cost: replaced by a conservative site.

**Simply Chocolate** (Spring/Summer, 2017). Each bar staged as a character you unwrap. Signature: the foil peel. Patterns: Interaction as Argument, Content-Derived Color. Cost: weak accessibility; works for a dozen products, not a catalog.

**The New Mobile Workforce** (Immersive Garden for Citrix, 2017). A dull B2B message told as a racing pit-lane story. Signature: one speed transition used at every break. Patterns: Metaphor Engine, Masked Cut (one branded transition). Cost: scroll-jacked linear navigation.
