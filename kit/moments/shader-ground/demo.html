shader-ground

An animated ground for a hero or a band, drawn by one WebGL fragment shader from the page's colour tokens. Scroll down to see it; it pauses when it leaves the screen.

A ground that moves slowly

Domain-warped noise flows between the background, primary and accent tokens. Variants change the character: flow, contour, grid and caustic. Without WebGL, under reduced motion or with JavaScript off, a CSS gradient made from the same tokens stays in place.

Below the ground the page continues as normal. The render loop stops while the section is off screen or the tab is hidden, and the Pause motion button stops it at any time.

Text over the ground sits on a token-coloured scrim when data-contrast="scrim" is set.
