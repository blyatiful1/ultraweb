particle-field

Several thousand soft points drift through a curl-noise flow field. When the section reaches the middle of the screen they gather into the word below, and they scatter again as it leaves. A fine pointer pushes them aside.

Drift

The word is real text in the page. With WebGL running it turns transparent and the particles draw it; without WebGL, under reduced motion or with JavaScript off, it stays visible over a dotted ground.

Below the field the page continues. The simulation stops when the section is off screen or the tab is hidden, and it draws fewer points when frames run long.
